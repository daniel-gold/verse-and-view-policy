# Privacy Policy for Verse & View

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Verse & View is a Chrome extension that displays daily Bible verses and scenic photography from the Holy Land on your new tab page. This policy explains what information the extension and its supporting services handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The extension uses Chrome's local extension storage to save:

- Your selected Bible translation and appearance settings, such as text size and background brightness.
- Verses you choose to save.
- Cached verses, photographs, and the available translation list, so content can load without downloading it again each time.
- A randomly generated installation identifier that distinguishes this installation of the extension in your browser profile.
- A previously configured username, if one is present in your saved settings.

The extension does not use Chrome's synchronized storage to sync this information across devices. Your saved-verse list and appearance settings are not uploaded to our servers. Your selected translation is sent when requesting verse content, and the installation identifier is sent with verse and translation-list requests.

## 2. Information received by our services

When the extension requests verses, available translations, or photographs, our services and hosting providers receive information needed to deliver that content and operate the service.

Our API request logs include:

- Your IP address and browser information sent in the user-agent header.
- The random installation identifier and extension version, when supplied by the extension.
- The requested date and Bible translation, and the translation and verse identifier returned, where applicable.
- Technical details such as the endpoint requested, request identifier, response status, processing time, and error or diagnostic codes.

Hosting services may also maintain standard access and diagnostic logs for API and image requests, including request times, IP addresses, browser information, and requested URLs. If a username was previously configured in the extension, it is sent with verse requests to select the corresponding verse schedule and may appear in hosting request logs.

The installation identifier is not your name or email address, but it lets us associate requests from the same installation. This information is not fully anonymous. Request records can reveal which Bible translations and verses were requested.

The extension does not require an account or payment. It does not read your browsing history, the contents of other webpages, or your existing clipboard contents. When you choose to copy a verse, it writes that verse to your clipboard.

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Deliver daily verses, translations, and photographs.
- Remember your settings and saved verses on your device.
- Understand service usage and performance, including requests from the same installation.
- Diagnose problems, maintain reliability, and protect the service against misuse.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Service providers and sharing

We use Google Cloud services to operate Verse & View. These include Cloud Run to host the API, Cloud Logging to store operational logs, Cloud Storage to deliver photographs, and Firebase/Cloud Firestore to store verse content, translation information, and schedules. Google processes information necessary to provide these services. Storing our content in Firestore does not mean that your locally saved verses or appearance settings are uploaded there.

You can read more about Google's practices in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you contact our Gmail support address, Google also processes that correspondence as our email provider.

This privacy-policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website is separate from using the extension, and website hosting providers may receive technical information about your visit. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 5. Storage, retention, and security

Locally saved information remains in your browser profile until it is replaced, removed by you, or cleared when the extension is uninstalled. Cached content may be refreshed or replaced during normal use.

Server logs are retained according to the retention settings of our hosting and logging services for operational, troubleshooting, and security purposes. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request. You may contact us for information about the retention applicable to your data.

The extension uses HTTPS to communicate with its API and download remote photographs. We use access controls to protect backend services and information. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can change your translation and appearance preferences in the extension's settings, and remove individual verses from your saved verses.

Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage and stops it from making further requests. Clearing its local storage removes its settings, saved verses, caches, and installation identifier. If you use the extension again after clearing its storage or reinstalling it, a new identifier may be created. These actions do not delete existing server logs or support correspondence.

The extension currently does not offer a separate switch to disable operational request logging while continuing to use its online features.

To ask about information associated with your use of Verse & View or request access or deletion, email [verseandview@gmail.com](mailto:verseandview@gmail.com). We may need additional information to locate relevant records and verify a request. Because the extension does not require an account, we may not be able to associate technical logs with a particular person. We will respond to privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Verse & View changes. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making changes to how information is handled.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact [verseandview@gmail.com](mailto:verseandview@gmail.com).
